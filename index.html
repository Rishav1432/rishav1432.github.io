✨ What is Efude?

Efude is a beautifully crafted drawing application designed specifically for Windows users. Whether you're creating manga, illustrations, or just sketching ideas, Efude gives you a crisp, responsive drawing experience right out of the box. It's completely free and open-source — meaning you can use it for any project, personal or commercial.

Built with Rust, Efude runs smoothly on most Windows computers without draining system resources. The app comes with a full set of illustration and manga tools, plus unique brushes that add character to your work. Everything is designed to feel natural and intuitive.

🖥️ System Requirements

Efude is designed to work well on most Windows computers. Here's what we recommend:

  • Operating System: Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit)
  • Processor: Any modern Intel or AMD processor (dual-core or better)
  • Memory: 4 GB RAM or more
  • Storage: At least 200 MB free space
  • Graphics: Integrated graphics are fine; a dedicated GPU is better

These are general guidelines. Efude is optimized to run efficiently, so even older computers can handle it comfortably.
